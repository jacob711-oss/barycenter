Every atom wants a certain number of bonds: hydrogen 1, oxygen 2, nitrogen 3, carbon 4. Molecules whose atoms are all satisfied are stable; a spark or a hot collision can break bonds and set off a chain reaction. Temperature is simply how fast the atoms jiggle.
